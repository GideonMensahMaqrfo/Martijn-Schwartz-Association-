About us

Success is built not just on strategy, but on the relationships we choose to nurture.

Your trusted partners

Martijn Schwartz & Associates is a boutique business law firm with extensive experience from large firms. Our goal is to provide premium transactional, corporate and securities counsel in a practical manner.

We cannot handle every matter, nor can we serve an unlimited number of clients. But we hope to have the opportunity to work with you, and that our passion for the practice of law makes us your counsel of choice.

Mission, vision and commitment

Our mission

To give companies, founders and investors premium transactional, corporate and securities counsel in a practical way, drawing on extensive experience gained at large firms.

Our vision

Justice is not a favour, it's a right. Fair representation should never be reserved for the privileged. It must be accessible, unwavering and rooted in truth.

Our commitment

We are committed to our clients, not their fees. True advocacy begins with understanding, not invoices, and we uphold every client's rights with clarity, compassion and dedication.

Why choose us

Client commitment is our cornerstone

From a new company to public company disclosure requirements, we support you through every stage of your business.

Flexible like your business

Protecting what matters most

Your business, your family and your peace of mind. With us you gain more than legal support: a partner who stands by you.
