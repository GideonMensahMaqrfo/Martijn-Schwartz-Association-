Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about working with us.

How do I book a consultation?

Use the form on our contact page, call the office nearest to you or email us. We reply within one business day.

Is the first callback free?

Yes. The first callback to discuss your matter is free and without obligation.

Can I meet you by video call?

Yes. Consultations can take place in person at any of our offices, by phone or by video call.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the matter. After your consultation you receive a written estimate of fees and costs before any work begins.

Can you help if my business operates in more than one country?

Yes. With offices in the United States, the Netherlands and Australia, we coordinate cross-border work so you have one point of contact.

Is what I tell you confidential?

Yes. Information you share with us is treated as confidential. Contacting us through this website does not by itself create a client relationship.

In which languages can I contact you?

You can contact us in English or Dutch.
