Privacy policy

How we collect and use the information you give us through this website.

What we collect

When you use our contact form we collect the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number, country, the office and practice area you choose, your preferred callback time and your message.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to provide legal services.

How it is sent

Form submissions are delivered to our email through a third-party form service. We do not sell or share your information for marketing.

Storage in your browser

This site stores your language choice and whether you closed the cookie notice. It does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing Info@wetheavelsadocate.com.
